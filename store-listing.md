## Full Description (4000 chars max)
De-Brill Learn is an interactive phonics and English pronunciation app designed specifically for Nigerian primary school learners aged 2 to 11.

Based on the acclaimed Blended Phonics and Mastering English Pronunciation book series by David Olufeagba, the app consolidates 8 books into one intelligent learning platform.

FEATURES:
- AI Pronunciation Coach - Coach Brill listens to your child speak and gives warm, specific feedback on their sounds
- Interactive Sound Chart - All 44 English phonemes with British English audio examples
- Book Library - Full content from Blended Phonics (Pre-Nursery to Nursery 2) and MEP (Primary 1 to Primary 5)
- Progress Tracking - Parents and teachers get clear summaries of learning milestones
- Offline Support - Download lessons for areas with limited connectivity
- NERDC Aligned - Follows the 2025 Nigerian curriculum framework
- British English default with American English option

DESIGNED FOR:
- Young Learners (ages 2-5): Blended Phonics series with large touch targets and animated characters
- Primary Students (ages 5-11): Full MEP series with IPA transcription and pronunciation drills
- Teachers: Class management dashboard and curriculum-aligned content
- Parents: Progress monitoring and daily learning summaries

De-Brill Learn is published by De-Brill Consults Ltd - a Nigerian educational consultancy with a proven track record in English language development for schools.
